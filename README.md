## 📌 Problem Statement
Standard navigation platforms exclusively optimize for transit speed and geographic distance, disregarding ambient air toxicity and personal carbon output. As a direct consequence:
- Pedestrians, cyclists, and school children are routinely routed along congested, high-emission arterial corridors during peak rush hours.
- Commuters lack visibility into their personal environmental impact and receive zero incentives to adopt lower-emission transit habits.

---

## 💡 The Solution
**Green-Path** transforms urban navigation into a health-first, carbon-conscious routing platform:
1. **Low-Exposure Eco-Routing:** Computes paths using localized air quality data, prioritizing routes that bypass severe particulate pollution pockets.
2. **Contextual AI Health Advisories:** Leverages Google AI Studio (Gemini) to evaluate route exposure duration and generate actionable recommendations for sensitive groups, schools, and parents (e.g., recess timing alerts, mask advisories).
3. **Gamified Carbon Tracking:** Quantifies emissions saved per trip against vehicle baselines and credits users with eco-points stored in cloud persistence.
